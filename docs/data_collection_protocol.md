# Data collection protocol — priorities for the next round

Evidence: `reports/alphabet_nested_2026-09-25/REPORT.md` (nested LOSO, 4 hauuto signers).

## Priority 1 — Level 1 tone marks (dấu sắc, huyền, hỏi, ngã, nặng)
Unseen-signer top-1 is 40.8% [7.5, 74.2] on tone marks against 85.6% on letters; signer tai reaches 10%.
Causes seen in the data:
- **Too few examples**: 6 clips per tone per signer (30 per signer), 4 signers.
- **Signers perform the tones differently**: median wrist travel for dấu hỏi is 0.09–0.10 palm lengths
  for hau/vy (finger movement only) and 1.1–1.5 for khoi/tai (whole-hand movement). With 3 training
  signers, a new style is often unseen.
- **Movement toward the camera** (tai's dấu nặng) is removed by the palm-scale normalisation.

What to record:
1. At least 10 signers, 10 repetitions per tone each, in 2 sessions (different day/room).
2. Write the reference form of each tone (source document + short video) into the protocol before
   recording, and ask signers to follow it. Keep a free "natural style" repetition as a separate label.
3. Keep the whole hand inside the frame for the full movement (tai's clips cut the fingers off).
   Hand about 1/4 of frame height, not covering the face.
4. Fixed 3 s clips, the sign starting after ~0.5 s of rest, so movement onset and speed are comparable.
5. Record the camera (model, resolution, fps) and the signer ID and hand dominance at capture time,
   never assigned later.

## Priority 2 — Level 1 letters
Letters are 85.6% [74.8, 96.4] on unseen signers (4 signers). More signers are the main need.
Add left-handed signers and webcams at other heights/angles (tai's hand roll −16° vs −2 to −10°).
